# Race plan: Shamrock then St. Anthony's

[[profile]] · [[plan-shamrock-2027]] · [index](../index.md)

Written 22 Oct 2026. Shamrock Half is Sunday 21 Mar 2027, 7:30 AM, 42nd and Atlantic, Virginia Beach. St. Anthony's Olympic is Sunday 25 Apr 2027, 6:50 AM, Vinoy Park, St. Petersburg. Swim 1.5 km, bike 40 km, run 10 km.

The floor at Shamrock is better than 1:29:18. Around 1:25 is the reach, and it is not the pace to open at. Sub-2 at St. Anthony's is the Olympic goal. Outdoor bike watts are UNKNOWN. Heart rate is the outdoor governor.

## Shamrock course

Published half, from the [organizer](https://www.shamrockmarathon.com/halfmarathon/): a loop, not a boardwalk out-and-back. North from the resort strip, tree-lined Shore Drive along First Landing State Park, then south to a boardwalk finish just past King Neptune. Start 7:30 at 42nd and Atlantic. Water about every 1.5 miles. Gatorade Endurance, lemon-lime, at those stops. GU at mile 5.5. The mile-19 GU is the marathon.

Elevation change on the half is described as about 20 feet (secondary course guide, not a survey in this file). There is no real hill. The Rudee Inlet bridge, miles 15 and 23, is the marathon. Do not pace the half as if that bridge is coming.

Fort Story was closed for the 2026 race, days before the start ([Virginian-Pilot, 18 Mar 2026](https://www.pilotonline.com/2026/03/18/shamrock-route-change/)). The 2026 half went out Atlantic to Shore Drive and back. Their 1:29:18 was that morning. Whether 2027 goes back onto the base is UNKNOWN until J&A publishes the map. The shape that is published is Shore Drive and the boardwalk finish. Recheck the map in February.

Wind is the hill. March is Virginia Beach's windiest month. At the 7:30 start across 12 past editions, the median temperature was 45°F, a warm year 54°F, rain at the start 2 of 12 years ([Wyndo](https://wyndo.app/races/shamrock-marathon), ERA5 at the start hour). Their own 2026 start was 54°F, 10 mph, dry. The usual March wind at Oceana is from the north ([Weather Spark](https://weatherspark.com/m/147082/3/Average-Weather-in-March-at-Virginia-Beach-Oceana-Naval-Air-Station;-Virginia;-United-States)). Northbound is then a headwind. The southbound boardwalk finish is then a tailwind, unless the wind flips, in which case the exposed last miles are the headwind. That is the mile that has ended PRs. People go out too fast in miles 1–2 because Atlantic Avenue is flat, crowded, and cool, and nothing makes them slow down.

## Shamrock pace

Open on the floor. 1:28:00 even is 6:43/mile, 4:10/km. That is 1:18 under 1:29:18. The 1:25 script is 6:29/mile, 4:02/km, and it is locked until the 11 Jan 10k is under 39:00. Their 2026 race averaged 178 bpm. If heart rate is already 178 at mile 4, the pace was too fast.

| Miles | 1:28 script | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–2, Atlantic heading north | 6:48–6:53 | Pack, no hill, often the headwind. Five seconds fast here is the mistake. |
| 3–7, Shore Drive | 6:43 | Trees. If the wind holds the pace at 6:50, keep the effort. Do not chase the watch. Heart rate stays at or under 176. |
| 8–11, heading south | 6:43 | If the wind was north, this is the free speed. Do not spend it. A fast split here gets paid back on the boardwalk. |
| 11–13.1, boardwalk to Neptune | 6:40 only if heart rate is still at or under 182. Otherwise hold 6:43. | Exposed. Last 400 m is the only kick. |

If the 10k unlocks 1:25, use the same shape five seconds per mile faster: 6:34, then 6:29, and the boardwalk only if heart rate is not already 184.

## St. Anthony's course

Official: Olympic 1.5 km, 40 km, 10 km, 6:50 AM, Vinoy Park ([RunSignup](https://www.trisignup.com/Race/Events/FL/SaintPetersburg/StAnthonys)). The [2024 bike map](https://satriathlon.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/St.-Anthonys-Triathlon-Olympic-Bike-Map-23-3002600_Final.pdf) is flat city streets. Mount on Bayshore, out First Avenue South to a turnaround at 28th Street, later a second turnaround at 70th and Pinellas Point, back past the Dali, dismount on Fifth Avenue Northeast. About 20 turns. Bricks near the Vinoy. Pavement is rough. There is no climb to pace. The bump is bricks and potholes.

The swim is in the bay off Vinoy. Wind and chop are the problem. In 2024 the Olympic swim was cut for safety. On 27 Apr 2025 the official water temperature was 77.5°F and wetsuits were legal. Their rule: wetsuits count for awards at 78.0°F or colder. A reading the morning of, about 5:30, decides it. Bring the suit. Decide at the beach, not the night before.

The run has a timed turnaround. Water and Gatorade every mile. Gels at mile 1.5 and mile 4.5 ([race rules](https://satriathlon.com/rules-usat/)). Late April at the gun is warm and humid, and it is warmer by the 10k. Heat, not elevation, is the run limiter.

People go out too hard in the first 200 m of the swim, in the first 10 minutes of the bike while passing people on the bricks, and in mile 1 of the run. That last one is how a 41-minute run becomes 44.

## St. Anthony's pace

Honest sum, not a fantasy. Swim 22:15, the band they have already raced (22:08 and 22:18). Transitions 2:15 together (ESTIMATED from the Rumpass gap between moving time and the reported finish). Bike 55:30 at heart rate 172–179. Run 41:00. Total 2:01:00. Sub-2 needs the bike closer to 54–55 and the run closer to 40:30, which is the Pleasants run, not a wish. Do not write 298 W on the bike. That was one race, on a different course, and outdoor FTP this block is UNKNOWN.

| Leg | Plan | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Swim, first 200 m | Easy, then settle | The bay start is a fight. A sprint here costs the bike. Target 22:00–22:20. |
| Bike, first 10 min | 10–15 bpm under the race heart rate | Bricks, turns, and the urge to pass. Heart rate 165 is fine. |
| Bike, the rest | 172–179 bpm | Pleasants was 179 for 56 minutes and the run survived. Rumpass was 177 at much lower power and the run was 44:00. Stay in this band. If heart rate hits 185 before halfway, back off. |
| Run, mile 1 | 6:50–7:00 | Legs lie for one mile off the bike. Their open 10k pace is 6:35. Do not open there. |
| Run, miles 2–6.2 | 6:35–6:45 if heart rate stays at or under 180 | That is the Pleasants 10k, not faster. Hold if the sun is up and heart rate is climbing. |

## Fuel

What they take now is UNKNOWN. The product gets chosen on the rehearsals below and then frozen. Not on race week.

A half at 85–90 minutes needs 30–60 g of carbohydrate per hour, started early, not a gel at mile 8. Two gels they have already used, about 20–25 g each, at 20–25 minutes and again at 45–50 minutes. Small sips of the drink they have trained. The course GU at mile 5.5 only if that exact GU is the one in the rehearsals. Otherwise carry it and skip theirs. Same rule for the lemon-lime Gatorade. Water if the drink is new.

St. Anthony's is about two hours. Once the gut has handled it, 60 g per hour from the first minutes of the bike through mile 2 of the run. On the bike that is one bottle with about 40 g, drunk across the hour, plus one gel at 25 minutes. One gel in the first mile of the run, before the course gel at 1.5. The mile-4.5 gel only if it is the same product. Do not jump to 90 g per hour. That rate needs glucose plus fructose and a gut that has practiced it. Theirs has not.

No caffeine gel unless a rehearsal already included it.

## Race weeks

Nothing new after Thursday. Shoes, gel, drink, breakfast, shorts. Thursday is the last day any of those can change.

Shamrock week. Thursday 18 Mar is the freeze. Carbohydrate load starts Friday 19 Mar at dinner and runs through Saturday. About 8 g per kilogram of familiar food, not a new pasta dish. The 12 Oct calendar tile said 143 lb, about 65 kg, so about 520 g if the weight is still there (MEASURED that week, not a race-week weigh-in). Sunday breakfast 4:45–5:00 AM, the same breakfast as the 21 Feb rehearsal, low fiber. Saturday is 15–20 minutes and four strides. No long run.

St. Anthony's week. Thursday 22 Apr is the freeze. Load from Friday 23 Apr dinner through Saturday only. A two-hour race does not need a three-day stuff. Saturday is packet pickup and bike check-in, noon to 5. No race-day packet pickup. No workout Saturday. Sunday breakfast about 4:00 AM, same as the brick rehearsal.
