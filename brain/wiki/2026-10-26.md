They asked about two changes. Not adopted.

Long run plus 5 minutes a week, to 110 minutes by mid-December. The step size is fine. An unbroken line is not. A 110-minute easy run is about 13 miles at 5:15–5:20/km. The same week as 4×8 minutes is about 21 miles (MEASURED arithmetic), over the 20-mile cap. 110 minutes belongs on a week with a short second run, not on a threshold week. One shorter long run every fourth week stays. The 13-mile touch stays in February, not as a new floor from December.

One quality run, then 4×8 minutes at 4:05–4:10/km by late November. One quality run is already the rule. 4×8 is the right workout and it is already dated 7 Dec, after the 5×6 minute weeks at 4:15–4:30/km. 4:05–4:10 is the Pleasants 10k pace from 4 Oct 2025, 4:06/km at 180 bpm, and that 10k was off the bike.
